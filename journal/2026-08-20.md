### Nine things broke, and they were all the same thing

Every single one was a mechanism that worked in basketball and had never once been
asked to work in another sport. Found by the founder playing, not by the gate.

1. **The Control Room's ESPN base was hardcoded to `basketball/wnba`** — so on a
   baseball night it searched the WNBA scoreboard for WSH and TEX, found nothing, and
   everything downstream died: autopilot never saw an inning end, Call It armed and
   watched nothing, Pull answers found no game.
2. **And it was hardcoded a second time**, in the AUTO block, so fixing the first left
   autopilot logging "Feed unreachable" every twenty seconds under a badge showing the
   game live.
3. **A round was assumed to be one period** — baseball is three rounds across nine
   innings, so autopilot pushed innings 1–3 during the 2nd.
4. **The same assumption in the runner**, which put "OT in progress" on every phone in
   the 4th inning of a nine-inning game. The fix for it had been written weeks earlier
   and had never run, because `periodLabel` could not see `AUTO` and a bare `catch`
   swallowed the ReferenceError.
5. **And a third copy of it in the Control Room**, found before it could overwrite the
   second. The comment above the broken line said "fix BOTH copies or neither".
6. **The player app looked `SPORT_CFG` up by family instead of league**, so every
   baseball room fetched its scoreboard from the WNBA feed. That is why the card read
   NOT STARTED over a game in the 6th.
7. **Caught It could never fire on baseball.** Freshness was decided by a sequence
   number that ascends in football and restarts every at-bat in baseball. Not rarely.
   Never, in silence, behind a green ARMED badge.
8. **A round opened for innings nobody played** — at a final whistle `periodDone` says
   yes to every period, so a 9-inning game ended holding an OT round worth 70 points.
9. **Following a link between rooms showed the other room's scored answers.** The
   football room displayed "Quarter 1 scored" before kickoff, `SF 0 LAC 1`, and four
   football questions answered *Single · One or two · Seven or more · Rangers* — the
   baseball key, character for character. `chooseGame()` was called from exactly one
   place in the file, the tile in the rail. A URL never reached it.

### What that pattern actually says

508 checks passed all week and not one of them had ever asked the host engine to run a
night in a sport that is not basketball. **Green meant green for basketball.** The
individual fixes matter less than that sentence.

So the work that counts from today is `qa/night-per-sport.js`: 82 checks across six
leagues, driving the real engine over real recorded feeds, hosting a full night in
each. Sabotage-tested against the actual bugs — putting baseball's rounds back to
`[1,2,3]` turns it red with *"periods [4,5,6,7,8,9] fall past the last round, so the
scoreboard calls them overtime"*, which is what four people watched.
