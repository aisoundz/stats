## Notes

### The biggest finding of the month, and it took three nights to see

**The last round of every game has been unreachable, in every sport, on every night.**

The ESPN feed flips to Final, and the runner opens the final round on its *next* poll.
Measured three times:

| | |
|---|---|
| 23 Aug · WNBA · IND@CHI | Final → Q4 opened **34s** later |
| 25 Aug · WNBA · POR@DAL | Final → Q4 opened **32s** later |
| 25 Aug · MLB · LAD@ATL | Final → 7th-9th opened **34s** later |

That consistency is the whole finding. **~32-34 seconds is one poll cycle**, so this is not
bad luck on certain games — it is structural and it happens every time. The runner behaves
correctly throughout: it sees Final, refuses to quit (*"the game is over but not every
quarter is scored — staying up"*), opens the round, and scores it minutes later. The round
genuinely exists and is answerable.

**The player app has already said goodbye.** `nightIsOver()` is `phaseNow()==='final'`, so
the instant the feed flips, the app renders the ending — while the Home tab simultaneously
shows a correct red banner reading "🔴 Q4 is open — tap to answer" that leads to a screen
saying "You finished on 149 points". Two surfaces, two sources, one of them guarded.

**And the round we lose is always the most valuable one.** Tonight's baseball plan is worth
30 / 50 / **70** — the unreachable round is **47% of the round points**. Basketball's Q4 is
40, also the highest. Anis: *"we havent had it yet when there is questions every quarter"* —
he has never once answered a final round. Some of the `0 pts` entries in Night By Night are
probably this.

**The fix already existed and almost nothing consulted it.** `nightRoundsOutstanding()` was
written 23 Aug after the first occurrence, is live, and its suite passes 38/0 against the
shipped build. It has **one** call site (`:23597`). Four other places decide the night is
over without it (`:9806`, `:14845`, `:26952`, and a direct `phaseNow()` read at `:8430`).
So the *settle* path was guarded and the *render* path was not. The fix is to give
`nightIsOver()` a single owner. **In flight, not yet deployed.**

This is the fourth variant of one-fact-many-copies found today, after `ROOMSTAT`,
`lastStand`/`#landingTip`, and the rail highlight.

### The prediction card was dead in production for two nights

`roundIsOpen()` shipped 24 Aug 07:39 (ruleset `c707bf5f`) and refused **every** prediction
card, because it requires a `rounds/{id}` document and nothing has ever created one for
`rP`. Proven in the live database: 24 Aug rooms took 7 round submissions from 3 seated
players and **zero cards**, against one card per player on all 28 nights before. That is
600 of the night's 1,000 points, silently refused, with three retries and no error surfaced.

It also took the anti-cheat with it — the card's lock is the *existence* of the submission,
so with creates denied a player could sign out and be handed a blank sheet after the buzzer.
Fixed today as `ba70839` / ruleset `0e46e0fa`, one additive line, verified against the live
release in both directions.

**The memory note said it was "written, compiles, NOT deployed; one command."** It had been
deployed for a day. Two sessions reasoned from that and it turned a live outage into a
misdiagnosis. When a note and a commit message disagree, read the live system.

### Tapping a player's name lost 43% of thumb taps

Not 1-in-8 as first measured — the loss window *is* the time the finger is on the glass:
7% at an instant synthetic click, **43% at 120ms**, 57% at 300ms. `buildPred()` rebuilds
every option via `innerHTML`, handlers were per-node `onclick`, and if the subtree swaps
mid-press **no click event is dispatched at all** — not at capture, not delegated. Chromium
loses it too, so it was never a Firefox quirk. It had been hiding inside a "flaky" test for
days; the test was the only thing telling the truth. Fixed as `.221`; the flake went 31% → 0.
