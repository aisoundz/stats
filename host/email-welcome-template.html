STATS GAMETIME is the game that pays to pay attention. There's a game on, you're watching it anyway, and we hand you a scoreboard for it. You make your calls before the first whistle, then the game itself asks you questions about what's happening while it happens. The closer you're watching, the better you do.

We built it to get you inside the game. It's a fun way to keep up with your teams, play along with your friends, and find out how much you actually caught. It's free to play, and there's no wager in it anywhere.

Play a practice round
No account needed. About 2 minutes, on whatever sport is up that day.
How a game night works
1 · Before the whistle
You fill in your card. Six calls on the game, things like who scores the most and how many rebounds a player pulls down. Lock it in and it's sealed for the night.
2 · Every quarter
A round of questions opens, and they're about what just happened in front of you. There's a clock on each one, and answering quickly is worth more than answering late.
3 · Caught It
This one arrives while play is live. Something happens, a question lands, and you have a few seconds to call it. It's the part that rewards keeping your eyes up.
What's playing

The WNBA, the NFL, MLB and MLS are live right now, most nights of the week. The NBA and the NHL are next, with more to follow, and our plan is a lot more sports by the time the Olympics arrive. Every question also plays in Spanish, and you can have them read aloud and answer out loud if you'd rather not look down.
