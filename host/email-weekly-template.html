What Got Built

The whole week went into one idea: this stops being a basketball game. Baseball and football had banks written and proven against live feeds weeks ago, but neither had ever opened a round with a person in it. Thursday both did. Friday ran three sports in an evening.

The one worth naming: practice handed everyone basketball, whatever they wanted. Five leagues were built and four of them were unreachable unless you knew to edit the address bar. There's a sport picker on the front page now.

And Spanish stopped being a basketball feature. Forty-six questions across baseball, football, soccer and hockey had no translation at all. A Spanish player picked baseball and got English mid-sentence. All of them are done.

The High

Thursday. First live baseball and first live football in fourteen game nights, two hours apart, and both held. Nothing dramatic happened, which is the point.

The Low

The home page told people “No game tonight” on a night with two games on it. Something picks a game of the night every single day and the page had never once read it, so it was counting down to a tip-off that had already happened, fourteen hours earlier.

It took three tries to fix. Each time I checked it on a clean browser and it looked fine, because a clean browser has nothing left over from the night before. The one phone it was broken on was mine. Three weeks in and I'm still checking my own work on the one machine where the bug cannot appear.

Three Things That’d Actually Help
1
 Play one this week. Three rooms a night from Monday, one per sport. About two hours, free.
2
 Try it in Spanish. Menu, Español. Every question in every sport is translated now. The app around them is about three-quarters done, and I want to know where it still shows through.
3
 Tell me the exact moment it felt off. Not “it was fine”. Every fix above started with somebody saying exactly that.
